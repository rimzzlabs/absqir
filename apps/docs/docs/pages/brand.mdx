# Brand

The absqir mark is the product's own mechanism drawn small: a QR-shaped path,
open at one corner, with a fresh module sliding into the gap — the rotating
token replacing the one before it. The module in the center is the reader.

## Color

| Token | Light | Dark | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#131A26` | `#E7ECF4` | The mark's path and modules, headings |
| Paper | `#F6F8FB` | `#10151E` | Grounds, tinted toward cobalt — never pure white or black |
| Cobalt | `#2563EB` | `#7CA9FF` | The incoming module, links, focus, one accent per view |

Cobalt marks the brand, not a state. Success, warning, and error keep their
own semantic colors inside the app.

## Typography

- **Wordmark**: JetBrains Mono 600, always lowercase: `absqir`. Never
  capitalize the name, in prose either.
- **Text**: Inter. Both faces already ship with the app; the brand adds no
  font.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the center module's width.
- Do not rotate, distort, outline, or add effects to the mark.
- Do not recolor the mark outside ink and cobalt.
- On photos or busy grounds, place the mark on a paper-colored tile first.
