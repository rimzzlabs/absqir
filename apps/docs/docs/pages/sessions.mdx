# Sessions and check-in

A session is one moment people are expected: a shift, a meeting, a
workshop. It has a start, an end, a late threshold, and one or more groups.
Everyone in those groups is on the list.

## The session clock

A session moves through three statuses on its own. Nothing is stored; the
status is read from the timestamps every time.

| Status      | When                                                                                                                |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `scheduled` | Before check-in opens.                                                                                              |
| `running`   | From `opens before` minutes ahead of the start until the end, or from the moment an organizer presses **Open now**. |
| `done`      | At the end, or when an organizer presses **Close now**.                                                             |

When a session becomes done, every expected person without a record gets an
`absent` record. That happens on the next read after the end, or at once on
a manual close, so no cron job is required.

## Statuses per person

| Status    | How it happens                                                                |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `present` | Checked in no later than `late after` minutes past the start.                 |
| `late`    | Checked in after that, while the session still runs.                          |
| `excused` | Set by an organizer by hand. Leave requests in the next phase set it as well. |
| `absent`  | Written when the session closes, for everyone expected who never checked in.  |

An organizer can change any status by hand from the session page. The record
remembers how it came to be: `screen`, `scanner`, `manual`, or `auto`.

## Two ways to check in

**The room screen.** The organizer opens the session's room screen on a
projector or a tablet. It shows a QR code that encodes a link with a token.
The token is an HMAC over the session id and the current 20 second window,
keyed by a per-session secret that never leaves the server, so a photo of
the code expires within seconds. A member scans it with the phone camera,
signs in if the phone asks, and is checked in. Only a signed-in member of
the organization can use the link, and only if they are on the list, unless
the session allows walk-ins.

**The scanner at the door.** Each member has a pass for the session on
**My sessions**: a QR code signed with the session secret and bound to that
person. The organizer opens the scanner page, points the camera at the pass,
and the person is in. A pass can be pasted as text when the camera is not
available.

Either way, the clock decides present or late. A second scan of the same
person changes nothing and says so.

## Schedules

A schedule is a rule: every weekday at 09:00 for 60 minutes, or every
Tuesday at 19:00. It carries its own late threshold, opening margin, groups,
and timezone, and spawns sessions two weeks ahead. The sessions appear when
anyone lists them, so a fresh rule shows its first sessions at once. Editing
a rule replaces the future sessions nobody has touched; the ones that ran
stay as they were.

## For members

**My sessions** lists what expects you, from yesterday on, with your record
once you have one and your pass while the session runs. **History** lists
every closed session with your status and an attendance rate. Nobody else
in the organization sees your history page.
