# Reports and calendar

<div role="doc-subtitle">Count what happened, and look at what comes next</div>

Two ways to look at what happened, and at what comes next.

## Reports

**Reports** counts the records of every event that starts inside a range.
Pick a range with the presets or the two date fields, and narrow it to one
group. The page then shows:

- totals for the range: how many events, how many closed, how many people
  turned up, and the split between present, late, excused and absent,
- one row per person, per group, or per event, with the rate for each.

Two rates appear:

<TwoRates />

| Rate       | How it is counted                                                                |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Attendance | Present plus late, over everyone judged. An excused person is left out entirely. |
| On time    | Present, over the people who turned up. It says how often somebody beat the late mark. |

A dash means nothing was judged yet, so no rate exists. Excused never counts
for the rate and never against it: leave is neither presence nor absence.

Absent records are written when an event closes. An event that is still
open therefore shows the people who arrived, and nobody as absent. The
totals say how many events in the range have closed, so the numbers are
easy to read.

## CSV export

**Download CSV** takes the table you are looking at, with the same range and
the same group filter. The file holds the counts and the rates for every
row. A single event also exports its own list from the event page.

Each cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is prefixed with an
apostrophe, so a spreadsheet reads it as text and never as a formula.

## Calendar

**Calendar** puts every event on a month or a week grid, Monday first. A
dot marks the status: grey for scheduled, green for running, faint for done.

A schedule only creates events a fortnight ahead. Browsing a later month
still shows what those rules will produce, marked with a repeat icon and
greyed out. Nothing is written for a projection; it appears as a real
event once the schedule reaches it.

Click a day number to open that day in full, with counts and groups. Click
the plus in a cell, or **New event on this day** in the panel, to create an
event that starts at nine that morning.
